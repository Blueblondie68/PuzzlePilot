# PuzzlePilot film quiz packs

- Audit each individual film from beginning to end. Cover the major story beats, character arcs and outcomes, principal and supporting cast, production, significant music, awards and nominations. Let the material determine the number of questions and omit padding.
- Use UK English, individual film titles, meaningful tags and reliable references for each question. Supply four distinct and plausible options with exactly one correct answer.
- Check wording and tested facts against the entire question bank, including retired and unconnected packs. Verify pack numbers and unused question IDs against the current branch head.
- When expanding a pack, retain its valid questions and IDs. Validate the pack files before committing to GitHub. Wait for the user's Render approval before connecting any new or expanded pack to the live quiz.
